## Traceability rules

Label high-priority features as one of: `PS` (direct problem statement), `PRODUCT` (SETU's chosen approach—upload-first source, progressive generation, offline doubt queue, reference imagery), or `QUALITY` (security, privacy, accessibility, reliability and tests). Do not claim the PDF mandates a specific LLM, upload requirement, image API or offline architecture.

## What must be measurable

- **Understanding:** multiple observations per concept, not an opaque AI score.
- **Adaptation:** a reasoned next action, prerequisite revisit or stretch task tied to evidence.
- **Teacher empowerment:** a teacher can inspect the reasoning and override it.
- **Indian context:** actual source/board/language metadata, low-bandwidth/mobile behaviour, truthful coverage.
- **Learn/Solve/Test:** real activity, persisted attempts and feedback, not static placeholders.
