### Threshold Standard
- **Cosine Distance Cutoff**: `(c.embedding <=> :vec::vector) <= 0.45` enforced directly in `retrieval.py`.
- **RRF Reciprocal Rank Cutoff**: Chunks must score `rank_score >= 0.01` with minimum 1 valid result.
- **Abstention Response**: When results are empty or below threshold, SETU returns `insufficient_evidence: True`, `citations: []`, and an honest academic refusal:
  > *"The selected document does not contain sufficient verified evidence to answer this question accurately. Please review the relevant textbook chapter or consult your teacher."*
